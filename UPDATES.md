# Prototype updates / 原型更新

## 2026-11-24 — Winding paths, larger moments

- A gently winding, rising lantern path leads from the garden to the concert entrance. Distant stage lights are visible beyond the gate.
- The Ado-inspired scene has a larger, more detailed water-emergence set piece. Part of its body stays below the surface.
- The LiSA-inspired scene has 15 distinct first-row characters, with individual clothing patterns and accessories. A close-up control cycles through the left, middle and right groups.
- Both scenes remain in one concert lab. Switch scenes, change seats, raise a light stick, or play your own local music. The bundled demonstration audio is an original lighting test, not either artist's recording.

庭院山路现在有缓弯和高低变化，门后能看到舞台灯光。水上魔王扩大并增加细节，下半身仍留在水中。另一场的前排应援团补至 15 人，并加入左、中、右三组近看按钮。

### Testing and limits / 测试与边界

Route clearance, stair height, scene switching, model cleanup, audio behavior and release files have automated checks. Browser testing also covers entering from the garden and phone-sized layouts. Real iOS and Android devices still need community testing; we do not claim universal smooth performance.

No commercial songs are included. Local music is not uploaded by the concert player. Concert crowds are simulated; the concert lab is not a multiplayer concert. These are unofficial fan experiments, not artist or rights-holder endorsements. The garden source remains private.

### Helpful feedback / 欢迎这些反馈

Please share your phone model, OS, browser, steps, and what you expected versus what happened. Screenshots are welcome; please hide personal information. Do not attach music files or other third-party assets. Suggestions and testing are voluntary, with no promise of payment or adoption.
